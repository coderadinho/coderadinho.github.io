ST449 · Reinforcement learning · Group project

Learning to play 2048

The question. How do value-function design, exploration and rewards change the way an agent learns to play 2048?

My contribution. I wrote the code and produced all the graphs for this group project, beyond drafting the problem formulation, methods section, and contributing across the final submission. The experiments compare afterstate temporal-difference learning with Q-learning, then vary exploration and reward design. TD-learning with afterstates did well, though it seems state representations affected performance far more than complex composite reward shaping.
